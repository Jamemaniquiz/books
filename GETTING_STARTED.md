# 🚀 GETTING STARTED WITH BOOKNEST

Welcome to BookNest! This is your complete book shop system. Let's get you up and running in 5 minutes.

---

## ✅ STEP 1: Open Admin (Seller Area)
- Click "Home" button at top
- You're now in the Admin area
- This is where YOU control everything

---

## ✅ STEP 2: Set Up GCash (So Buyers Can Pay)

### What you need:
- Your GCash phone number (e.g., 09171234567)
- Your GCash QR code (screenshot from your GCash app)

### How to set it up:
1. Scroll down to "💳 GCash Payment Setup" section
2. Enter your GCash phone number
3. Click "📷 Upload QR Code" button
4. Select your GCash QR code image
5. You should see a preview of the QR code
6. Click "💾 Save GCash Details"
7. See confirmation: "✓ GCash details saved successfully!"

**Done! Buyers can now pay you!**

---

## ✅ STEP 3: Add Your First Book

### What you need:
- Book title
- Author name
- Price (how much you want to sell it for)
- How many copies you have
- A picture (optional but helps sales!)

### How to add a book:
1. Click "Inventory & Sales" button at top
2. Look for the form at the top with fields:
   - Title
   - Author  
   - Genre
   - Price
   - Stock
   - Type
   - Condition
3. Fill in the details
4. Click "+ Add Book"
5. You'll see "✓ Book added successfully!"

**Book is now in your inventory!**

---

## ✅ STEP 4: Add a Picture (Optional)

### How:
1. Find your book in the table below
2. Look for "📷 Photo" button
3. Click it
4. Choose an image from your computer
5. Wait for it to upload
6. Done!

**Picture helps customers see what they're buying!**

---

## ✅ STEP 5: Make Book Visible to Buyers

### This is IMPORTANT:

1. Find your book in the table
2. Look for "➕ Add Shop" button (scroll right to see it)
3. Click "➕ Add Shop"
4. Button changes to "🛒 In Shop"
5. Now buyers can buy it!

**Without this step, buyers can't see your book!**

---

## ✅ STEP 6: View Your Shop

### To see how buyers see it:
1. Click "🛒 View Shop" button at top right
2. You should see your book displayed!
3. Try adding to cart
4. Try checkout process
5. Verify your GCash QR code appears

**Perfect! You're ready for business!**

---

## 📱 WHAT HAPPENS WHEN BUYER SHOPS

1. **Buyer opens shop**
   - Sees your book with picture, title, author, price
   
2. **Buyer adds to cart**
   - Clicks "Add to cart"
   - Can add multiple books
   
3. **Buyer checks out**
   - Fills in name, contact, address
   - Chooses payment method (GCash recommended)
   - Clicks "Place order"
   
4. **Buyer sees payment details**
   - Order ID generated
   - Your GCash QR code shown
   - Your GCash phone number shown
   
5. **Buyer pays you**
   - Scans QR code with GCash
   - Or sends money to your number directly
   - Uses Order ID as reference
   
6. **You receive payment**
   - Money arrives in your GCash instantly
   - Mark order as "Paid" in admin
   - Prepare to ship

## Auto-refresh behavior (2026-09-11)
BookNest no longer polls seller/store pages on a timer. Pages render once on load and update only when Supabase Realtime reports an actual shared-data change. Manual Refresh buttons remain available where provided. The cart countdown remains a local 1-second timer because it updates the hold timer, not the page.
