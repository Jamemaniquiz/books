Buyer rules, shipping & layaway guide

Everything you need to know before you check out — how to pay, how shipping works, and how layaway lets you pile up books and pay for them over time. Please read this fully before placing an order.

💳 GCash, payment first 📦 J&T Express or TikTok Shop 📅 Layaway from ₱1,000
💳

1. Payment

We only accept GCash, and payment is required first — once we confirm your transfer, we reserve your books for you. You'll see our GCash number and QR code on the Payment info tab and again at checkout.

📦

2. Shipping options

J&T Express

Standard shipping. Books are payment first via GCash; the shipping fee is computed and paid separately via GCash once we total your order.

TikTok Shop checkout

Cash on delivery (COD) is available for shipping fee only — books themselves are still payment first via GCash. We only rent our TikTok checkout link, so a non-refundable ₱20 rental fee is added to cover its use.

ImportantThe ₱20 TikTok checkout rental fee is non-refundable once your order is placed.
📝

3. Shipping details we need

To prepare your invoice, please send your full name, contact number, and complete address. Double-check every detail before sending — we are not responsible for orders sent to the wrong address because of information you provided.

🚚

4. Order & shipping process

  1. Choose shipping or pickup. Pickup orders can be held for up to 1 month.
  2. Send your shipping details. Fill in the name, contact, and address section accurately — this is needed for J&T orders.
  3. Pay the shipping fee. For J&T orders, this is paid separately via GCash after we compute your total.
  4. Pay before the deadline. If the shipping fee isn't paid by the deadline, the order will be cancelled.
⚠️

5. No cancellations & disclaimer

  • No cancellations or changes once payment has been made.
  • Shipping schedules depend on the rider — please wait for their update rather than ours.
  • Once a package is handed to the courier, we're not liable for damage, loss, delay, or any other issue caused by the courier. This is part of our agreement with you.
  • All books are pre-loved unless stated otherwise.
📅

6. Layaway option

Layaway is available on orders of ₱1,000 and up. Keep adding books to your cart in the shop — as soon as your total reaches ₱1,000, a Layaway panel unlocks under your cart. Pick a duration and you'll immediately see exactly how much to pay right away, plus every payment still to come and when it's due, before you check out.

DurationLayaway fee
1 month₱100
2 months₱200
3 months₱300

The fee is added on top of your books' price. Your first payment is due the moment you reserve; any remaining payments are split evenly and fall due on the 15th of each following month — the shop shows your exact schedule before you confirm.

Layaway rules
  • Miss a payment and you have 1 week to catch up.
  • If it's still unpaid after that week, the layaway is cancelled and any payments already made are non-refundable.
  • A late fee of ₱15 per day applies once you're past the due date.
  • Down payments and the layaway fee are non-refundable if the plan is cancelled.
  • No cancellations or changes once a layaway has been confirmed.
  • Once fully paid, your books are packed and sent to you.
💛

7. General reminders

  • Always send proof of payment.
  • Double-check your shipping details before sending them.
  • Please be patient and kind — we're a small bookstore run by real people.
  • We appreciate your support, and follow us on Facebook for new arrivals and selling schedules.
Thank you for supporting our small bookstore. Happy reading, bookworms! 🤍
